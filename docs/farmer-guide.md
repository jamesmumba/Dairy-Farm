# Dairy Farm: farmer's guide

A short guide to keeping your farm records on your phone.

## 1. Getting started

1. Install **Dairy Farm** and tap **Create an account**. Enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters).
2. Type the **6-digit code** we email you. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.
3. Answer a few questions: your name, your farm, your currency (ZMW or USD), and which reminders you want. You can turn on **app lock** so the app opens only with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN.
4. First steps:
   * **Sales › Milk prices:** set your price per litre.
   * **Sales › Milk stock › Opening balance:** how much milk you have now.
   * **Animals › Register an animal:** add your cows. The ear tag is optional. Every animal gets a short ID such as `3F9A2C` that never changes.

## 2. Every day

* **Milking:** go to *Records › Milking round*. Pick morning, afternoon or evening, type the litres beside each cow, and tap *Save round*. Made a mistake? Open the entry, tap *Correct*, and say why.
* **Selling milk:** go to *Sales › New sale*. Choose the customer, then the litres (1, 2, 5, 10, 20 or Other), and how much they paid. Check the summary and save. The app won't let you sell more milk than you have. If the stock is wrong, record the missing milk or make a **stock adjustment** with a reason.
* **Later payments:** go to the customer and tap *Record a payment*. Payments clear their oldest sales first, and anything extra is kept as credit.
* **Milk not sold:** use *Records › Milk use* for milk used at home, fed to calves, spoiled or given away.

## 3. Animal health

* Record a **health concern**, a **vet visit** or a **treatment** from the cow's page.
* **Milk withdrawal:** when the vet gives a medicine that needs milk withdrawal, turn on *Milk withdrawal required* and enter the **exact end date and time from the vet**. Until then:
  * the cow shows a red warning
  * her milk is recorded but **not for sale**
  * the milking round warns you.

  Finishing the treatment early does not end the withdrawal.
* The app records what you and your vet decide. It does not diagnose or suggest medicines or doses.

## 4. Breeding and calving

* Record **heat**, **mating** or **insemination**. A pregnancy check is planned automatically (you can change the number of days in Settings, so ask your vet).
* A cow is marked **pregnant only after a pregnancy check** says so.
* When she calves, record the **calving**. You can register the calf at the same time.

## 5. Vaccinations

* Add your vet's **vaccination schedules** (*Records › Vaccination schedules*).
* Record a dose for one animal, or use **Group vaccination** for many at once.
* Reminders come 30, 7 and 1 days before a dose is due (or the days you choose).

## 6. Reminders and Home

**Home** shows what needs attention, the herd, milk, sales and money, and what's coming up. **Reminders** lists withdrawals, overdue and upcoming care, unpaid balances and stock problems. Phone notifications arrive at your chosen time, even without internet.

## 7. Working without internet

Everything works offline. Your records are saved on the phone and sent to your account when you're connected. The bar at the top tells you which:
* **Synced:** everything is saved to your account.
* **Saved on this phone, waiting to sync:** it will go as soon as you have a connection.
* **Needs attention:** your account did not accept a change, for example a duplicate ear tag or not enough milk in stock. Tap the bar to see why, and fix or discard the change. Nothing is lost until you choose.

Don't sign out while changes are waiting. The app warns you if you try.

## 8. Reports and sharing

*More › Reports* covers herd, milk, health, breeding, vaccination, and sales, money and stock, for any period. Tap **Share PDF** or **Share CSV** to send a report. Share only with people you trust, because reports contain your farm and customer records.

## 9. Your account

* **Change password:** *More › Security*.
* **History of changes:** *More › Audit history*.
* **Delete your account:** *More › Delete account*. We will contact you before anything is deleted. Export your records first.

## Need help?

Contact the support email in the app store listing. Never share your password with anyone, including support.
